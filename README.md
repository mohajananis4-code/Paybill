PAYBILL

Speak It. Bill It. Pay It.

An AI-powered multilingual voice billing and payment platform.

💡 The Idea

Creating bills manually can be time-consuming, especially for small shop owners and businesses. Language barriers and manual data entry can make the process even more difficult.

PayBill transforms spoken words into digital invoices using AI.

Instead of typing every product name and quantity, sellers can simply speak. PayBill understands the request, prepares the bill and makes payment easier.

🚀 What Makes PayBill Different?

- Voice First: Create bills by speaking instead of typing.
- Multilingual: Supports Tamil, English and Hindi voice input.
- AI Powered: Gemini AI identifies product names and quantities from speech.
- Seller Controlled: Sellers review items and enter their own prices.
- Smart Calculation: Automatically calculates the invoice total.
- Digital Payment: Integrates PayPal Sandbox for payment testing.
- Digital Receipt: Generates a payment receipt that can be downloaded.

⚙️ How PayBill Works

01 — Speak

The seller selects a language and speaks the product details.

02 — Understand

AI processes the recognised speech and extracts product names and quantities.

03 — Verify

The seller reviews the extracted information and enters the correct prices.

04 — Calculate

PayBill calculates the total amount automatically.

05 — Pay

The customer completes the payment through PayPal Sandbox.

06 — Receive

A payment receipt is generated and made available for download.
